# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 29, 2026  

Welcome to **The Haunted Arcade** (Ghost Gaming Portal). We respect your privacy and are committed to ensuring a secure, transparent, and completely private gaming experience. This Privacy Policy outlines how our web-based arcade operates and explains our data practices.

---

## 1. Zero Personal Data Collection

The Haunted Arcade is a client-side, browser-only gaming portal. **We do not collect, harvest, track, sell, or transmit any personal information.**

- **No User Accounts:** You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to play any of our titles.
- **No Analytics or Trackers:** We do not embed third-party tracking pixels, advertising networks, telemetry monitors, or commercial profiling cookies.
- **No External Data Transfers:** All game logic, rendering, and audio synthesis run directly on your device inside your web browser.

---

## 2. Local Browser Storage (`localStorage`)

To provide a seamless arcade experience, our games may utilize your browser's built-in **Local Storage (`window.localStorage`)** or session storage. 

This storage is used **strictly and exclusively** for:
- Saving your personal high scores, lap times, and drift points locally.
- Remembering your sound preferences (e.g., Mute/Unmute audio state).
- Preserving current stage progression or unlocked loadouts.

**Important:** All data saved in `localStorage` remains completely on your local computer or device. It is never uploaded to any remote server or shared with any third party. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings or developer tools.

---

## 3. Zero Third-Party Services & Zero External URLs

- **100% Self-Contained:** The portal does not load any external third-party scripts, stylesheets, fonts, trackers, or CDNs. All assets and typography use local system fonts and local files.
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** The arcade is 100% ad-free, tracker-free, and independent of any third-party infrastructure.


---

## 4. Children’s Online Privacy

Our games are designed for general audiences and comply with the spirit of the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because we collect zero personally identifiable information from any user, children can enjoy our browser games in a safe, unmonitored environment.

---

## 5. Changes to This Policy

If we update our privacy practices or introduce new local storage features, any revisions will be posted directly to this document.
